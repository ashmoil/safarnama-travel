How it works

Let’s plan your journey

Four simple steps from “I need a break” to “I’m on the mountain”.

Tell us your budget, dates and travel style on a free 15-minute call or WhatsApp. We shortlist destinations that actually fit you — not what is easiest to sell.

Within 24 hours you get a day-by-day itinerary with hotels, transfers and experiences. Change anything as many times as you like before you confirm.

Pay in easy instalments via UPI, card or net-banking. Every booking comes with instant confirmation vouchers and a transparent price breakup — no hidden charges.

Your personal trip manager stays on WhatsApp 24×7 during the journey — from late check-ins to last-minute plan changes, we have got you covered.
